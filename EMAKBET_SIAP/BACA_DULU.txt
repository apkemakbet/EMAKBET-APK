==================================================
  LANGKAH 5: BIKIN KEYSTORE (buat tanda tangan APK)
==================================================
Di komputer (perlu Java/JDK terpasang), buka CMD:

  keytool -genkey -v -keystore emakbet.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias emakbet

Isi password (CATAT!), nama, kota, dll.

Ubah ke base64:
  Windows PowerShell:
    [Convert]::ToBase64String([IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("emakbet.jks")) > keystore_base64.txt

Isi keystore_base64.txt -> dipakai di GitHub Secret
